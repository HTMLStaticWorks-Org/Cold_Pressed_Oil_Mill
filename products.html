Available Container Sizes & Storage Instructions

Unrefined wooden chekku oil contains natural seed waxes and fine plant solids that respond to light and air. Choosing the right container format and storage routine keeps your oil fresh from the first pour to the last.

250ml

250 ml & 500 ml Glass Bottles

Ideal for small households, trial sampling, and specialty oils such as Sweet Almond Oil and Castor Oil.

1 Litre Artisanal Glass Bottle

5 Litre & 15 Litre Food-Grade Tin Cans

Light-proof metal tins designed for joint families, restaurants, caterers, and retail refill stations.

Storage Care for Chekku Oils

  • • Store bottles and tins in a cool, dark cupboard away from stove heat and direct window sunlight.
  • • Always use a dry pouring spout or spoon; water droplets can shorten shelf life.
  • • Fine seed sediment at the bottom of the bottle is a natural hallmark of unrefined gravity-settled oil.
