About Marachekku Naturals

Dedicated to Traditional Wooden Chekku Oil-Making

MARACHEKKU NATURALS is a traditional cold-pressed oil mill focused on a single craft: extracting unrefined culinary and household oils using seasoned wooden chekku presses.

Our oil-making philosophy rests on simplicity and transparency. Rather than chasing industrial speed or high-temperature extraction yields, we work with whole, sun-cured regional oilseeds—groundnuts, black and white sesame, sulfur-free copra, mustard seeds, castor beans, and specialty kernels—pressing them in small weekly batches.

Every batch is strained through unbleached cotton cloth and left to settle naturally by gravity before being bottled in glass or sealed in food-grade bulk tins. We do not use chemical solvents, artificial fragrances, synthetic anti-foaming agents, or neutralising washes.

Ingredient Selection

We inspect every sack of groundnuts, sesame, copra, and mustard for dryness and cleanliness—rejecting broken or damp kernels before pressing.

Vagai-Wood Pressing

Our wooden mortars and pestles crush seeds slowly at ambient room temperature so the oil keeps its natural colour, viscosity, and seed aroma.

Honest Clarification

We rely on unbleached gada cotton straining and 48 to 72 hours of sunlit gravity settling rather than chemical bleaching earths.

Direct Mill Supply

Serving both individual households with 500 ml & 1 L glass bottles and commercial kitchens, retailers, and distributors with 5 L & 15 L tins.

Our Guiding Standard

The Four Truths of Wooden Chekku Oil

Our mill practices remain anchored in traditional South Indian oil-pressing wisdom passed down through generations.

Zero Refining Chemicals

Never subjected to phosphoric acid, caustic soda, or deodorising steam washes. What you taste is pure cold-pressed oil.

Glass & Tin Packaging

Bottled in dark and clear food-grade glass or food-safe tins to protect delicate polyphenols and keep oil aromatic.

Slow Vagai Ulakkai

The seasoned vagai timber naturally absorbs heat and resists friction, keeping temperatures strictly below 45°C.

Fresh Small Batches

We press weekly according to actual demand so that bottles delivered to your door are freshly pressed and rested.
